Metric Definitions & Methodology

Weekly Window

The weekly window starts on Monday 00:00:00 UTC and concludes on Sunday 23:59:59.999 UTC. Dates are strictly matched against UTC database timestamps to ensure timezone neutrality.

Completed This Week

Reflects the exact count of coursework items whose completed_at timestamp falls between the start and end of the current calendar week.

Weekly Completion Percentage

Calculated as completed / (completed + pending_due_this_week) * 100. This reflects your progress specifically against the commitments scheduled for this week.

Overdue Classification

An assignment is marked overdue when its due date and time is earlier than the current instant and its status has not yet transitioned to COMPLETED.
